Accounts and children

Anyone 13 or older can create an account. Children under 13 must be signed up by a parent or guardian, using the parent's email address and with the parent's permission; the parent confirms the account from their inbox before it can be used.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, a first name or nickname, and the year of birth (used only to check whether a parent's permission is needed).
  • Learning data: words added, favourites, folders, books added to the Smart Reader, reading position, notes, quiz and game results, and app settings.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We never see or store card numbers.

How we use it

Only to run My Word Book: signing you in, saving your learning, managing your free trial and subscription, and providing the AI voice and AI help. We don't sell personal information and we don't show ads.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase — sign-in and account storage.
  • Netlify — hosts the website.
  • OpenAI — turns text into speech and answers reading questions. Only the text needed for the request is sent.
  • Stripe — subscription payments.
