🎙️ Voice pre-fill

Private page for the owner of My Word Book. It makes the AI voice audio for every word in the dictionary once and saves it on your Netlify site. After that, every child hears the saved clips and you aren't charged again. You can stop at any time and continue later; words already saved are skipped for free.

1. Admin password

The PREFILL_TOKEN you added in Netlify → Site configuration → Environment variables (make up any long password). This is not your OpenAI key.

2. Which voices?

Each voice is a separate set of clips. Start with the voice the app uses most (Nova is the default).
